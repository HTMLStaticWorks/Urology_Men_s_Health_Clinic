COMMON QUESTIONS & CLARIFICATION

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about booking private appointments, patient confidentiality, consultation preparation, and clinic access.

How do I schedule a confidential specialist consultation?
You can submit our confidential booking form above, call our clinic reception directly at +91 00000 00000, or email hello@example.com. Our coordinator will contact you discreetly via your preferred communication method within 24 hours to confirm your appointment.
Is my consultation and medical record kept 100% confidential?
Yes, complete discretion is our highest priority. All patient consultations take place in private soundproof suites, digital health records are encrypted, and appointment scheduling ensures zero waiting room overlap.
What should I bring or prepare for my first appointment?
Please bring a valid photo ID, any recent laboratory test results or diagnostic imaging reports (if available), and a list of current medications. Arriving 10 minutes prior allows for seamless, unhurried registration.
Can I request a specific specialist for my consultation?
Yes, you may choose your preferred doctor when submitting the booking form — Dr. Arjun Mehra (Urological Specialist), Dr. Rahul Sen (Men's Health & Vitality), or Dr. Kiran Malhotra (Male Fertility & Andrology).
Where is private parking located at the clinic?
Reserved patient parking is available on Level B1 of the building. Dedicated elevator access leads directly to our private clinic reception corridor for full discretion.
What payment methods and insurance coverage are supported?
We accept all major credit/debit cards, UPI payments, and digital bank transfers. Itemized medical invoices are provided for direct insurance claims and reimbursement.
