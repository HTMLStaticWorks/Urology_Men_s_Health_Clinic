SPECIALIST CARE AREAS

Comprehensive Urological Services

Every care pathway is tailored to your individual clinical evaluation and privacy needs.

Urinary Health

Support for concerns such as frequent urination, urgency, difficulty urinating and other urinary symptoms.

Focus: Bladder function & urinary flow assessment

Prostate Health

Evaluation and management discussions for prostate-related concerns including benign enlargement and screening.

Focus: BPH, PSA test review & symptom management

Kidney & Bladder Health

Specialist assessment for relevant kidney and bladder conditions, structural evaluations and chronic care planning.

Focus: Infection management & organ health review

Stone Care

Evaluation and treatment planning for urinary stone-related concerns, metabolic analysis, and recurrence prevention.

Focus: Stone risk review & preventive hydration plans

Male Sexual Health

Private consultations for men's sexual health concerns, erectile health, vascular assessment and confidential support.

Focus: Vascular, nerve & psychological factors

Men's Hormonal Health

Evaluation of relevant symptoms like persistent fatigue, low vitality, and specialist guidance where appropriate.

Focus: Endocrine symptom review & metabolic advice
